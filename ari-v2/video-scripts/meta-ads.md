# Meta Ads: 30 second video

**Service:** Meta Ads
**Target length:** 30 seconds
**Word count:** 90

## Script

> Two recent Meta results. A New Zealand festival pre-event sprint:
> purchases up two hundred and sixty-five percent, reach up one
> fifty-five, sixty-seven thousand dollars in revenue at a fifty-five
> dollar CPA. A UK B2B shelter manufacturer: six thousand pounds of
> Meta spend over five months, closeable leads in a sector most
> agencies will not touch.
>
> The process is creative-first, targeting-second. Brief the creative
> against the audience we want, test variants until the algorithm
> has a clear winner, then scale carefully. Then hold the line.

## What this script proves

- Two named results: NZ Festival (+265% purchases, $67k at $55 CPA)
  and UK Shelters (GBP 6k delivering qualified B2B leads)
- B2C and B2B coverage in one breath
- A repeatable four-beat process (brief, test, scale, hold)

## Visual cues

- Open with steady eye-line, slightly forward energy
- Small pause between the two case results
- Confident close on "hold the line"
