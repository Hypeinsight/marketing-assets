# TikTok Ads: 30 second video

**Service:** TikTok Ads
**Target length:** 30 seconds
**Word count:** 86

## Script

> TikTok rewards content that looks like TikTok. Studio creative
> loses. Native creator content wins. That is the entire game.
>
> My process starts with the creative brief, not the targeting.
> We map the hook, the proof, and the call-to-action onto three
> native ad units, run Spark Ads on the creator's handle where it
> makes sense, and let the algorithm find the audience.
>
> Measurement runs through the pixel, not platform reach. Done well,
> TikTok becomes the cheapest performance media a serious brand
> can buy.

## What this script proves

- A clear principle (native beats studio) backed by a concrete
  three-part creative process
- Measurement discipline (pixel-based, not vanity)
- An honest, specific positioning ("cheapest performance media")
