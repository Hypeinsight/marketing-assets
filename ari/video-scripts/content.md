# Content & Editorial Strategy: 30 second video

**Service:** Content
**Target length:** 30 seconds
**Word count:** 90

## Script

> Content done properly is the cheapest moat a business can build.
> For Selerity, one hero article pulled ninety thousand plus organic
> visits, seven minutes average time on page, a forty-three percent
> bounce rate. One article.
>
> My process is the inversion of most content shops. Pick the
> question your buyer is actually asking. Write the definitive
> answer. Not a thin post on the same topic everyone else is writing.
>
> We measure compounding traffic, time on page, and assisted
> conversions. That is how content earns its keep, not how many
> posts go live.

## What this script proves

- A specific, verifiable result (Selerity: 90k visits, 7 minutes
  on page, 43% bounce from one piece)
- A clear process that signals craft over volume
- Three measurement metrics that signal a results-focused
  discipline

## Visual cues

- Slow, considered delivery; a writer talking about writing
- Brief emphasis on "One article" so the leverage point lands
- Land "not how many posts go live" with quiet, confident
  eye-line
