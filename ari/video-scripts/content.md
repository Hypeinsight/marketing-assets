# Content &amp; Editorial Strategy: 30 second video

**Service:** Content
**Target length:** 30 seconds
**Word count:** 87

## Script

> Content is the cheapest moat a business can build. It just takes
> patience and a point of view.
>
> Most companies publish without either. Generic blog posts ground
> out for traffic, written by committee, optimised for keywords
> nobody actually searches.
>
> The content that earns links, ranks, and converts has a strong
> voice and answers a real question. One deep piece beats ten
> thin ones.
>
> If you're publishing and not sure it's working, the problem
> usually isn't volume. It's that the content isn't worth reading.

## Visual cues

- Slow, considered delivery; this is a writer talking about
  writing, so the tone should match
- Pause on "patience and a point of view" / they are the two
  load-bearing words in the script
- End with a quiet smile; the final line is a gentle provocation
