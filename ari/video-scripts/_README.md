# Ari profile page: service videos

Seven 30-second scripts, one per service specialisation on the Ari
profile page. Each one is written for direct camera delivery: short
sentences, conversational rhythm, no jargon for its own sake, and
ending on a clear next-step.

## Target spec

- **Length:** 28 to 32 seconds (target ~30)
- **Word count:** 75 to 90 words (assumes ~160 wpm conversational pace)
- **Format:** landscape (16:9) preferred for the player; vertical (9:16) also works
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum
- **Audio:** lavalier or shotgun mic strongly preferred over built-in
- **Framing:** mid-shot, eye-line camera, clean background, good light
- **Wardrobe:** consistent across the seven so they read as a series
- **Lower-third:** none needed on camera (the page already shows the
  service label and Ari's name in the card chrome)

## Delivery notes

- Read once for understanding, then deliver. Reading word-for-word
  on camera always sounds like reading.
- Keep the tone direct and conversational. Avoid superlatives.
- Land the final sentence with confidence, not a question lift.
- One take that flows is better than three takes that are perfect.

## File naming when you publish

Save the finished videos as MP4 to:

- `google-ads.mp4`
- `hubspot.mp4`
- `ai-agents.mp4`
- `seo.mp4`
- `geo.mp4`
- `content.mp4`
- `website.mp4`

Drop them into `ari/videos/services/` and the page will pick them
up automatically (the placeholder swaps to the real video on the
matching service card).
