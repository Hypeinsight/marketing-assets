# Meta Ads: 30 second video

**Service:** Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram / Messenger)
**Target length:** 30 seconds
**Word count:** 86

## Script

> Meta ads work when the creative does. Targeting plus creative is
> the formula. Get one wrong and the other cannot save you.
>
> Most accounts I audit are over-targeted and under-tested.
> Audiences sliced too thin. Two ad variants when there should be
> twenty. Advantage Plus turned off because someone read a blog
> post in 2023.
>
> Open the targeting, brief native-feeling creative, run more
> variants than feels reasonable, then let the algorithm do its
> job. That is the modern Meta playbook.

## Visual cues

- Even pace; the opening line is the hook
- Slight smile on "someone read a blog post in 2023" so the
  critique reads as observational, not condescending
- Land "let the algorithm do its job" with confidence and a
  small pause before the cut
