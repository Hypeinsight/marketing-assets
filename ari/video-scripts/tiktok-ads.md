**Service:** TikTok Ads
**Target length:** 30 seconds
**Word count:** 85

## Script

> TikTok rewards content that looks like TikTok. Brands that paste
> their YouTube cuts into the platform lose. Brands that brief
> native creators win.
>
> Three things matter here. Hook in the first two seconds. Native
> feel through the whole clip. Spark Ads on the creator's handle,
> not yours.
>
> Skip those and you are paying premium CPMs to be ignored.
>
> If you want a Reddit-honest read on whether TikTok is right for
> your business, send me a note. Sometimes the answer is no.

## Visual cues

- Light energy throughout; TikTok talk should feel current
- Brief pause after "lose" before "Brands that brief native
  creators win" so the contrast hits
- "Sometimes the answer is no" with a quiet smile; the honesty
  is the credibility
