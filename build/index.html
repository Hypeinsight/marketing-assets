i What we build

Two disciplines, under one roof.

Most agencies split design, engineering, and marketing across separate silos and hand the deliverables through three layers. We do not. One operator scopes the work, designs the interface, wires the code, and stays on the account after launch.

Discipline 01

Website builds, from wireframe to launch.

Conversion sites for businesses that need the website to earn revenue, not sit still. Editorial marketing pages, e-commerce storefronts, service portfolios, and internal tools that look and feel deliberate.

  • Discovery and conversion mapping
  • Custom design in Figma, prototyped end to end
  • Hand-coded builds (no templates) or headless CMS where it earns its keep
  • Analytics, SEO foundations, and tracking wired in at launch
  • Post-launch care so the site keeps working past week one
Forty-plus shipped. Categories from healthcare to hardware to hospitality.
Discipline 02

Micro-SaaS builds, owned outright.

Small, purpose-built applications that automate one clear job inside a business. Not enterprise platforms with a hundred features. Focused products that pay back inside a quarter and stay easy to maintain.

  • Product discovery: the one job to automate, and the numbers that prove it
  • Product design, prototype, and interactive walkthrough
  • Full engineering: front-end, API, database, auth, billing where relevant
  • Integrations with the platforms your team already runs
  • Deployment on infrastructure you control, not our proprietary stack
Our own product Arvi Health runs on this pattern (see below).

Four steps, no theatrics.

A build proposal is a promise about time, money, and scope. The steps below are how we keep that promise.

01
Discovery & scope
One call, one written brief. Business goal, target user, must-have features, budget envelope. You get a fixed scope and price by end of week one.
Week 1
02
Design & prototype
Figma prototype the whole surface before writing a line of code. You click through it, we iterate, we agree. No surprises at build hand-over.
Weeks 2-3
03
Engineering & launch
Custom code (or headless CMS where it earns its keep). Staging preview by week five. Launch when the analytics fire, the forms work, and the load times are honest.
Weeks 3-6
04
Post-launch care
Two weeks of live-fire support included. Optional retainer beyond that for updates, feature adds, or ongoing engineering. You own the code either way.
