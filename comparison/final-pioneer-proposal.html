Capturing High-Intent Leads

The New Zealand property market presents unique opportunities for targeted lead generation. Targeting Strategy

Audience Segmentation

Multi-layered targeting to reach the right buyers at the right time

Active Home Buyers

Ages 25-65 showing recent interest in property purchasing, engaged with real estate content, searching in your service areas.

Property Investors

High-net-worth individuals interested in investment properties, rental yields, and portfolio expansion. Finance and business publication readers.

First-Time Buyers

Ages 25-40 showing interest in first-home purchase content, mortgage information, and property guides. Requires nurturing.

Note: Seller acquisition campaigns available as an optional add-on if required.
