Strategic Overview

Strategic Overview

The New Zealand property market presents unique opportunities for targeted lead generation. With rising interest in property investment and the shift towards digital property discovery, Meta's sophisticated targeting capabilities allow us to reach qualified prospects at critical decision-making moments. Our strategy focuses on capturing high-intent leads through multiple campaign types, nurturing them through the sales funnel, and seamlessly integrating them into your CRM for follow-up. ---

Campaign Strategy and Execution Plan

Campaign Strategy Execution Plan

  • **Active Home Buyers**: People aged 25-65 who have shown recent interest in property purchasing, have engaged with real estate content, or are in areas with high property search activity. We'll target those searching for properties in your service areas with specific price points.
  • **Property Investors**: High-net-worth individuals and experienced investors interested in investment properties, rental yields, and portfolio expansion. We'll use interest targeting around investment property forums, finance content, and business publications.
  • **Home Sellers**: Homeowners who may be considering selling, identified through life events (moving, renovations, family changes) and engagement with property-related content. We'll target those with indicators of relocation or life transitions.
  • **First-Time Buyers**: Younger demographic (25-40) showing interest in first-home purchase content, mortgage information, and property guides. This segment often requires more education and nurturing.
