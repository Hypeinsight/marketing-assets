How Secure Is Your Website Right Now?

This quiz evaluates your website across 5 security dimensions: software maintenance, hosting quality, access & authentication controls, security configuration, and monitoring readiness. It's designed for business owners — no technical expertise needed. Score each statement: Yes = 2 pts  ·  Unsure = 1 pt  ·  No = 0 pts. Maximum 50 points. Results save automatically.

40–50: Strong
28–39: Moderate
15–27: At Risk
0–14: Critical
Strong (40–50) — Your website has professional-grade protections in place. Maintain monthly software updates and review access credentials annually.
Moderate (28–39) — Core protections exist but meaningful gaps remain. One unpatched plugin or reused password is all a hacker needs to gain entry.
At Risk (15–27) — Your site is a realistic and attractive target. Automated scanners actively look for sites with this profile. Professional attention within 30 days is strongly advised.
Critical (0–14) — Almost no active defences are in place. A breach may already be occurring silently. Immediate professional review is urgent — every day of inaction increases the risk.
