ii What we build

The support agent patterns we ship

Most support deployments stack two or three of these. We start with the highest-volume use case to build trust and prove deflection, then expand.

Conversational Triage Agent

First touchpoint on your help widget. Identifies the customer, classifies the query, attempts resolution from your knowledge base, and only escalates when needed, with a full context handoff.

Lower cost per ticket
Order & Account Agent

"Where is my order?" "How do I update my card?" The agent reads your order management system, accesses billing, and answers with real data. Most queries resolve in one message.

Up to 60% deflection
Resolution Agent

Beyond answers, the agent acts. Refunds within your policy thresholds, shipping address updates, subscription pauses. Backed by audit logs and human-in-loop for higher-value decisions.

Full-cycle resolution
Knowledge-Sync Agent

Reads new product docs, help articles, and policy updates nightly. Re-indexes the vector database so the support agents always answer from current information, never stale FAQ pages.

Always current
Voice-of-Customer Agent

Continuously synthesises support transcripts, reviews, and survey data. Surfaces systemic issues the moment they emerge, not in next quarter's NPS report. Product and ops get an early warning.

Issues caught early
Agent QA & Coaching

Reviews every human-handled ticket against your standards. Flags coaching moments, identifies training gaps, drafts kudos messages. Your support managers stop spot-checking and start improving.

iv How it ships

The deployment in three phases

Support deployments need extra care because customers see the output immediately. We sequence the rollout to build trust, prove deflection, then expand the agent's scope.

i
Weeks 1 to 3
Shadow deployment

The agent reads incoming tickets and drafts proposed replies. Your team reviews and edits before sending. We learn what your tone of voice is, where the agent gets it right, where it slips.

ii
Weeks 4 to 8
Confident topics live

The agent resolves clearly defined topics (order status, account changes, shipping) end-to-end. Everything else still routes to humans with the agent's draft attached as a starting point.

iii
Month 3 onward
Full autonomous scope

Refunds within thresholds, subscription changes, complex resolution paths. Confidence-threshold escalation. Continuous evaluation against a golden dataset of customer-rated interactions.

A note on integration We work with your existing help desk: Zendesk, Intercom, Front, Help Scout, Freshdesk, Salesforce Service Cloud, Kustomer. The agent layer plugs in as a tier-one assistant, with full ticket lineage preserved for your reporting.
v What you can measure

The four metrics that prove it worked

We design every support deployment with these four KPIs wired in from day one. They are what makes the business case durable across budget cycles.

  • 01

    Deflection rate

    Percentage of tickets resolved by the agent without human touch. We target 40 percent in the first 90 days, scaling to 70 to 80 percent within a year as topic coverage widens.

    Target: 70%+ within year one
  • 02

    Time to resolution

    From customer message to confirmed resolution. Agents typically compress this from hours-to-days down to seconds-to-minutes for in-scope topics, with customer satisfaction holding or rising.

    Target: under 2 minutes
  • 03

    CSAT and first-contact resolution

    We watch these like hawks. If automation comes at the cost of customer experience, the wrong topics are being automated. Both metrics should hold or climb post-deployment.

    Target: flat or rising CSAT
  • 04

    Cost per resolved ticket

    The final ROI number. Total fully-loaded support cost divided by total resolved tickets. Most deployments cut this in half within six months, often more.

    Target: 50% reduction
