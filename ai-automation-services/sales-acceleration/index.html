ii What we build

The sales agent patterns we ship

Every engagement is bespoke. Most fall into one of these patterns, deployed individually or orchestrated together as a connected sales engine.

Lead Enrichment Agent

Every new lead is automatically researched across LinkedIn, the company website, recent news, and your CRM history. The agent compiles a one-page brief: who they are, what they likely need, and a personalised hook for your reply.

Less guesswork per call
Intent-Scoring Agent

Reads form submissions, email replies, and website behaviour to assign a real-time intent score. Sales reps see only the prospects most likely to convert this week. No more cold-list grinding through dead leads.

Higher win rate per hour
Inbound Reply Agent

A lead submits a form. The agent researches them, drafts a hyper-personalised reply that references their context, and either sends within five minutes or leaves it in your drafts folder for one-click review.

5-minute response, 24/7
Outbound Cadence Agent

Multi-step outreach sequences that adapt to each prospect's behaviour. Reads replies, classifies intent, schedules follow-ups, escalates hot leads to a human rep. Generic templates retire forever.

Personalisation at scale
Pipeline Hygiene Agent

Reads your CRM nightly, flags stalled deals, suggests next steps, drafts re-engagement emails, identifies which deals will not close this quarter. Forecast accuracy improves without anyone touching a spreadsheet.

Forecasts you can trust
Meeting Prep Agent

Thirty minutes before every sales call, the agent compiles a briefing: recent news about the prospect, their last interactions, talking points, likely objections. Your reps walk in prepared, every time.

Better calls, lower CAC
iii Before and after

The same sales team, two months in

A real pattern we see across deployments. Same reps, same product, same market. The difference is the agent infrastructure quietly running in the background.

Before

Inbound dies on weekends Leads submitted Friday night are answered Monday morning. Most have already booked a demo with a competitor by then.
Outbound is generic Templated sequences that mention everyone's company name. Reply rates hover around 1.5 percent. Reps are demoralised.
Reps waste hours on research Each call requires 20 minutes of prep digging through LinkedIn and the prospect's website. Only the best reps actually do it.
Pipeline data is stale CRM updates happen Friday afternoon, if at all. Forecast meetings are guesswork. Sales leadership flies blind.

After

Every lead replied to in five minutes The inbound agent runs continuously. Reply is personalised, references the prospect's context, and either sends or sits in drafts for one-click review.
Outbound feels human Each prospect receives a sequence built from their actual profile and triggers. Reply rates double or triple. Reps focus on conversations, not typing.
Briefings appear automatically Thirty minutes before each call, the rep has a one-page brief ready. Prep happens in two minutes, not twenty.
CRM updates itself Post-call summaries, action items, and next-step suggestions written by the meeting agent. Forecasts get accurate. Pipeline becomes a real instrument.
iv How it ships

The deployment in three phases

Sales agents do not require a six-month transformation. We design the first agent to land inside two weeks, with measurable outcomes from week one.

i
Weeks 1 to 2
Pilot agent

Pick the leakiest part of your funnel (usually inbound response). Deploy a single agent. Establish baseline metrics: response time, reply rate, conversion to meeting.

ii
Weeks 3 to 8
Connected sales stack

Add lead enrichment, intent scoring, and meeting prep. Connect to your CRM. Train your reps on the new workflow. Tune prompts based on real interactions.

iii
Month 3 onward
Orchestrated sales engine

Outbound cadences, pipeline hygiene, post-call automation. Agents hand off to each other and to your reps. Continuous evaluation against your golden dataset of best closes.

A note on integration We work with the CRM you already have, not the one we wish you had. HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Microsoft Dynamics. If your stack has an API or a Zapier connector, your sales agents can read and write to it from day one.
v What you can measure

The four metrics that prove it worked

Every sales agent we ship has a measurement plan attached. These are the four we look at first to confirm an agent is paying back its keep.

  • 01

    Lead response time

    From form submission to first personalised reply. We aim for under 10 minutes, 24 hours a day. The conversion-rate impact compounds immediately.

    Target: under 10 minutes
  • 02

    Reply rate on outbound

    Personalised, agent-drafted outbound typically lifts reply rates from 1 to 2 percent up to 5 to 8 percent. We track this weekly per rep and per campaign.

    Target: 3 to 5× baseline
  • 03

    Qualified meetings per rep

    Hours reclaimed from research and admin convert directly into more sales conversations. The volume rises while the rep's working week stays flat.

    Target: +50% within 90 days
  • 04

    Forecast accuracy

    When CRM hygiene improves, forecast variance drops. Sales leadership gets to plan against real data, not optimism. Margin calls become possible.

    Target: within 10% variance
