i The case

The cost of decisions made on partial data

Every business already has the data needed to make better decisions. The problem is the data is scattered across systems no human can read all at once. Decision agents read across the silos and surface the signal that matters, in time to act on it.

Most decisions in most businesses are made on a fraction of the available data, by a tired human, late on a Friday. Decision agents are not about replacing judgement. They are about presenting full context the moment judgement is required. Why we built this practice

The decision agent patterns we ship

Every business has a different decision surface. The patterns below are the ones that have repaid their build cost fastest across our engagements.

Pricing Intelligence Agent

Monitors competitor pricing, demand signals, and your own inventory levels. Recommends or autonomously executes price adjustments within your guardrails. Margin protected, share grown.

Higher AOV, protected margin
Inventory & Reorder Agent

Reads sales velocity, supplier lead times, and seasonal patterns. Reorders autonomously within thresholds you set. Stockouts down, holding costs down, manual planning hours reclaimed.

Fewer stockouts, less capital tied up
Document Intelligence Agent

Contracts, invoices, reports, RFPs. The agent extracts the data points that matter, flags exceptions, summarises the rest. Lawyer-hours and analyst-hours fall, accuracy rises.

80%+ accuracy lift
Marketing-Spend Allocation Agent

Reads your ad platforms, attribution data, and historical performance. Suggests or executes budget reallocations daily. CAC down, ROAS up, fewer "what if we paused this channel" debates.

Better CAC, sharper attribution
Anomaly & Alert Agent

Continuously monitors KPIs, transaction patterns, and behavioural data. Flags outliers in real time with context, not just dashboards. The team finds the leak before it becomes the headline.

Issues caught hours earlier
Forecast & Scenario Agent

Runs rolling forecasts and what-if scenarios on demand. "What happens to cash if we slow Q3 hiring?" The agent reads finance data and answers within seconds with confidence intervals.

iv How it ships

The architecture that makes it work

Decision intelligence depends on data plumbing as much as on the agent itself. We sequence the build around the data foundations, then layer the agent on top.

1
Data foundations
Connect the systems the agent will read. CRM, ERP, ad platforms, analytics, financial systems. Clean enough to act on, governed enough to trust.
Weeks 1 to 4
2
Retrieval & reasoning
Vector databases for unstructured data (contracts, reports). RAG pipelines so the agent's reasoning is grounded in your real numbers, not its training data.
Weeks 3 to 6
3
Decision execution
The agent recommends, then executes within your guardrails. Confidence thresholds. Human-in-loop for high-stakes calls. Audit trail on every decision.
Weeks 5 to 10
+
Continuous evaluation
A golden dataset of historical decisions used to test the agent weekly. Drift caught early. Model and prompt updates ship without breaking what works.

v What you can measure

The four metrics that prove it worked

Decision agents pay back differently to support or sales agents. The KPIs we instrument from day one are different too.

  • 01

    Decision latency

    From signal to acted-upon decision. Pricing changes, reorders, budget shifts. Most decisions compress from days or weeks down to hours or minutes.

    Target: 10× faster
  • 02

    Decision accuracy vs baseline

    The agent's recommendations tested against historical decisions and outcomes. Where the agent disagrees with past human calls, we examine whether the human or the agent was right.

    Target: above human baseline
  • 03

    Direct financial uplift

    Margin protected on pricing decisions. Carrying-cost reduction on inventory. CAC reduction on marketing-spend allocation. The line items the CFO actually cares about.

    Target: clear net positive in 90 days
  • 04

    Hours reclaimed for strategic work

    The hours your senior team spent on data wrangling, now spent on judgement work. The qualitative side of the ROI but often the most durable change.

    Target: 20+ hours/week per senior
